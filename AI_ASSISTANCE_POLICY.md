# AI Assistance Policy

The academy trains both **independent competence** and **professional AI-assisted engineering**.

- **Learning mode:** AI may explain concepts, ask questions, or critique your explanation. Do not use it to replace the primary learning task.
- **Practice mode:** Unless the exercise says otherwise, do the first serious attempt without generated solution code. Use documentation and error messages first.
- **Closed assessment mode:** No AI-generated solution code, architecture, or answers. Official documentation is allowed unless the assessment forbids it.
- **Professional project mode:** AI tools are allowed, but you remain responsible for design, code review, tests, security, evaluation, and explaining every important decision.

A project you cannot debug without prompting is not yet strong portfolio evidence.
