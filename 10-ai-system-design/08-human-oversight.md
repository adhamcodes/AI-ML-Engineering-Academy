# Module 10.8 — Human Oversight & Decision Rights

## Capability
Place humans where uncertainty/consequence/authority requires them.

## Core model
Human-in-loop design needs clear review context, authority, SLA and fallback; “human review” cannot be a vague checkbox.

## Practice
Design escalation thresholds and review UI data.

## Debug / transfer
Avoid automation bias by showing uncertainty/evidence.

## Evidence to save
Decision-rights table.

## Mastery
You can explain who is accountable for each action.
