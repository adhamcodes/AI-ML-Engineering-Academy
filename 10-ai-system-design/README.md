# Phase 10 — AI System Design & Professional Engineering

**Typical effort:** ~4–6 weeks

## Purpose
Integrate ML/LLM capability with product requirements, distributed-system tradeoffs, risk and technical communication.

## Modules
1. Requirements & Success Metrics
2. Rules vs ML vs LLM Decision
3. Data Architecture
4. Batch, Streaming & Real-Time Patterns
5. Latency, Throughput & Cost Budgets
6. Reliability & Failure Modes
7. Security, Privacy & AI Risk
8. Human Oversight & Decision Rights
9. Architecture Communication & ADRs
10. System Design Boss Fight

## Gate
Pass **System Design Ready** by defending and revising a complete architecture under ambiguous/changeable constraints.
