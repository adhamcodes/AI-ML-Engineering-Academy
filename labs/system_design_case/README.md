# AI System Design Case

Design an AI support assistant for a company where answers may affect refunds and account access.

Your design must state:

- requirements and non-goals,
- data flow and trust boundaries,
- retrieval/model/tool boundaries,
- latency and cost budget,
- failure modes,
- evaluation strategy,
- human escalation rules,
- privacy/security controls,
- observability,
- rollback/degradation plan.

Defend at least two rejected alternatives. The grade is trade-off reasoning, not diagram beauty.
