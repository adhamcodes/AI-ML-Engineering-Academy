# Phase 1 Assessment — Math Ready

**Mode:** no AI-generated solutions. Calculator/Python may be used only where the question explicitly permits it.

Demonstrate algebra/functions fluency, vector/matrix operations and geometric interpretation, conditional probability/Bayes reasoning, expectation/variance intuition, derivatives/partial derivatives/chain rule, and gradient-descent reasoning. At least half the problems are ML-flavored transfer questions rather than copied exercises.

## Pass
You must explain the meaning of the quantities and recover from mistakes. Memorizing formulas without being able to choose or interpret them does not pass. Use targeted remediation for weak areas and re-test those areas.
