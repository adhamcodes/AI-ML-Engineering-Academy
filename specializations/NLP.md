# NLP

## Enter after
Complete the broad core or demonstrate equivalent mastery, especially Deep Learning, Transformers, production fundamentals and the relevant prerequisite area.

## Direction
Deepen linguistic/text tasks, retrieval, embeddings, sequence/transformer models, evaluation, multilingual/domain adaptation and production NLP systems.

## 90-day specialization rule
Choose one serious project/reproduction, one advanced primary resource, one codebase/community to read or contribute to, and one explicit mastery question. Do not begin three specializations simultaneously.
