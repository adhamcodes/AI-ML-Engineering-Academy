# Module 3.11 — Decision Trees

## Capability
Understand recursive feature splits, impurity/error reduction, overfitting, depth/min-sample controls, and piecewise decision boundaries.

Trees handle nonlinear interactions and do not require feature scaling in the same way as distance/linear methods.

Single trees can overfit and be unstable; ensembles address some weaknesses.

## Practice
Train trees at increasing depth, visualize one small tree, compare train/validation performance, and explain one path as human-readable rules.

## Mastery
You know what a split means and why an unconstrained tree can memorize noise.
