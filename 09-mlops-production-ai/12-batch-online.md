# Module 09.12 — Batch vs Online Inference

## Capability
Choose execution mode based on freshness, latency, volume and cost.

## Core model
Batch amortizes work and tolerates delay; online serves individual requests under latency/availability constraints. Hybrid designs are common.

## Practice
Design both modes for three use cases.

## Debug / transfer
Identify expensive online architecture for nightly job.

## Evidence to save
Architecture choice memo.

## Mastery
Choice follows product SLO and economics.
