# Phase 6 — Transformers & LLM Foundations

**Typical effort:** ~6–8 weeks

## Purpose
Understand modern language models deeply enough to reason below high-level APIs: tokenization, attention, transformer blocks, objectives, decoding, inference and adaptation.

## Modules
1. Subword Tokenization
2. Vocabulary, Embeddings & Output Heads
3. Queries, Keys, Values & Self-Attention
4. Multi-Head Attention
5. Positional Information & Causal Masking
6. Transformer Block Anatomy
7. Encoder, Decoder & Encoder–Decoder Families
8. Language-Model Objectives & Pretraining
9. Decoding & Sampling
10. Context, Inference & KV Cache
11. Hugging Face Model Workflow
12. Fine-Tuning & Parameter-Efficient Adaptation
13. Tiny Transformer Lab
14. Transformer Foundations Assessment

## Gate
Pass **LLM Foundations Ready** through implementation, tracing and debugging—not terminology recognition.
