# Module 11.11 — Choose a Specialization

## Capability
Select depth direction after broad trunk based on evidence, interests and market—not fear of missing out.

## Core model
Specialization narrows project/research depth while the broad trunk remains usable.

## Practice
Score six paths by interest, demonstrated strength, opportunity and prerequisite cost.

## Debug / transfer
Avoid choosing a title because of hype alone.

## Evidence to save
90-day specialization plan.

## Mastery
You can name what you will intentionally *not* study next.
