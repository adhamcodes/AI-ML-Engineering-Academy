# Module 11.9 — No-Degree Evidence Strategy

## Capability
Compensate for missing credential signal with stronger inspectable proof and targeted applications.

## Core model
Some employers filter by degree; others value equivalent experience/projects. You cannot control filters, but you can increase evidence density and widen channels.

## Practice
Build skills-to-evidence matrix for target roles; identify missing proof.

## Debug / transfer
Do not fabricate credentials/experience.

## Evidence to save
Resume/portfolio emphasizing shipped systems, contributions, measurable work.

## Mastery
Every major resume claim is verifiable.
