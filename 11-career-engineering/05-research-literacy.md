# Module 11.5 — Research & Paper Literacy

## Capability
Read papers for problem, method, assumptions, evidence and limitations without worshipping novelty.

## Core model
Paper reading is selective: abstract/figures/experiments first, then methods/math as needed. Reproduction can be partial.

## Practice
Summarize paper as claims/evidence/open questions; reproduce one small result if feasible.

## Debug / transfer
Spot benchmark/data leakage or unfair baseline caveat.

## Evidence to save
Research note/reproduction repo.

## Mastery
You can explain what paper actually established.
