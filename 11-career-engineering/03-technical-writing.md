# Module 11.3 — Technical Writing

## Capability
Explain systems, experiments and tradeoffs clearly.

## Core model
Writing reveals thinking and creates evidence beyond code. Good technical notes separate claim, method, evidence, limitations and next steps.

## Practice
Write 800–1500 word project/experiment note.

## Debug / transfer
Cut unsupported marketing language and vague “improved performance” claims.

## Evidence to save
Published/local article with reproducible figures.

## Mastery
Claims map to evidence.
