# How to Use This Academy

## The rule that matters most
Advance because you can **pass the gate**, not because you consumed content.

## Normal module workflow

1. Read the capability goal and prerequisites.
2. Study the assigned primary material.
3. Attempt active practice before collecting more explanations.
4. Complete debugging and transfer work.
5. Build the mini-build/project when assigned.
6. Attempt the checkpoint under its stated assistance rules.
7. Save evidence and record weak competencies.
8. Repair weaknesses before the phase gate.

## Time
Suggested durations are planning ranges, not deadlines. A phase can take longer or shorter depending on prior knowledge and gate performance.

## Notes
Keep notes compact. Prefer mental models, diagrams, mistakes, experiments, and decisions over copied definitions.

## AI assistance
Use AI as a tutor when allowed, not as a substitute for the capability currently being tested. Closed-assistance gates remain meaningful even when no software enforces them. See `AI_ASSISTANCE_POLICY.md`.

## Evidence
For major work, preserve the problem, your approach, code, tests/evaluation, failures you diagnosed, tradeoffs, and what you would change. This becomes both a learning record and later portfolio evidence.

## Studying with another learner
Work through the same module in parallel, but attempt exercises and closed assessments independently. Compare after submission. Code review and explaining different solutions are encouraged; copying removes the diagnostic value of the academy.

## When you get stuck
1. restate the problem in your own words
2. inspect prerequisites
3. reproduce the failure in the smallest case
4. consult the assigned reference/documentation
5. use allowed hints/tutoring
6. record what the failure taught you

Do not turn every difficulty into another course.
